# 04 — Competitor & Public-Knowledge Landscape

**Legend:** **[V]** = verified against a primary/reputable source (official docs, GitHub API metadata, major news outlet, academic paper). **[C]** = community claim (blogs, Medium, X threads, marketing sites) — treat as unconfirmed color, not fact.

## 1. Market-structure primer (what everyone else is trading against)

- **[V]** Polymarket resolves its 15-minute (and later 5-minute) crypto price markets via **Chainlink Data Streams** (low-latency signed price reports) + **Chainlink Automation** (on-chain settlement at the preset end timestamp) on Polygon. Announced Sept 2025. Sources: [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/370444/polymarket-turns-to-chainlink-oracles-for-resolution-of-price-focused-bets), [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/web3/2025/09/12/polymarket-connects-to-chainlink-to-cut-tampering-risks-in-price-bets), [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/polymarket-just-made-bitcoin-bets-settle-instantly-with-chainlink-upgrade/).
- **[V]** Polymarket's own real-time data socket exposes two external price feeds to all comers: **Binance spot** (`btcusdt`) and **Chainlink** (`btc/usd`) — i.e., every participant can watch the same reference prices we deliberately exclude from our scope. Source: [Polymarket docs — RTDS crypto prices](https://docs.polymarket.com/developers/RTDS/RTDS-crypto-prices).
- **[V]** **Dynamic taker fees introduced Jan 2026** on 15-minute crypto markets, explicitly to kill latency arbitrage. Fee is maximal near 50/50 odds (~3.15% on a 50c contract) and decays toward the extremes; collected fees are redistributed daily to makers via the Maker Rebates Program. Source: [Finance Magnates](https://www.financemagnates.com/cryptocurrency/polymarket-introduces-dynamic-fees-to-curb-latency-arbitrage-in-short-term-crypto-markets/).
- **[V]** Liquidity rewards: any wallet posting competitive resting limit orders in eligible markets earns from a rewards pool (>$5M/month platform-wide by 2026); crypto-market taker fees rebated ~20% to makers daily. Sources: [Polymarket docs — Liquidity Rewards](https://docs.polymarket.com/market-makers/liquidity-rewards), [Maker Rebates Program](https://help.polymarket.com/en/articles/13364471-maker-rebates-program).
- **[C]** Since the Chainlink integration, fast crypto markets reportedly did **$4B+ combined volume**, with $200M+ in week one of 5-minute markets and ~3x growth in platform daily volume. Source: [Cryptonews](https://cryptonews.com/news/chainlink-oracles-polymarket-volume-surge/) (couldn't fetch full text; figures from search snippet).

Implication: the entire public competitive game is anchored on the Binance/Chainlink reference feeds that our protocol excludes by design ([`strategy-research-protocol/SCOPE.md`](../../SCOPE.md)).

## 3. Public writeups & claimed edges

- **[C] Moonsat's taxonomy of ~1,000 profitable up/down bots** ([Medium, Jul 2026](https://moonsat.medium.com/6-main-types-of-trading-bots-on-up-down-markets-on-polymarket-12893a484f28), follow-up ["Inside the Mind of a Polymarket BOT"](https://coinsbench.com/inside-the-mind-of-a-polymarket-bot-3184e9481f0a)): (1) rebalancing arb — buy Up+Down when sum < $1 (example wallet `0xb27bc932bf8110d8f78e55da7d5f0497a18b5b82`); (2) directional-tilted arb; (3) fair-value repricing vs spot lag; (4) cross-timeframe 5m↔15m lag capture (example wallet `0x3a847382ad6fff9be1db4e073fd9b869f6884d4`); (5) order-book imbalance / legged two-sided structures; (6) near-resolution sniping at ~0.99. Claimed common traits: limit-orders-only, many small hedged edges, structure over prediction.
- **[C] "gabagool" style** ([Phemex News](https://phemex.com/news/article/polymarket-trading-bots-yield-510k-daily-profits-on-bitcoin-options-52075), sourced from an X post): buy whichever side gets "unusually cheap" (<35c triggers), DCA down, target blended cost 96–98c per winning $1; claimed $5–10k/day and 95–98% win rate on 15m BTC. Win rate is meaningless without tail-loss accounting — this is short-vol harvesting.
- **[C] Dump-and-hedge** ([Teraus, Medium, Mar 2026](https://medium.com/@Teraus/polymarket-15-minute-dump-and-hedge-bot-89018d7aa8fb)): buy the side that just dumped, hedge the opposite side once combined cost < target, lock the $1 payout spread.
- **[C] Latency arbitrage lore**: [QuantVPS](https://www.quantvps.com/blog/binance-to-polymarket-arbitrage-strategies) claims Polymarket books lag Binance by "30–90 seconds" on large moves and sells Amsterdam VPS proximity (5–12ms to the CLOB in London vs 130–150ms from US East); the latency-bot repo claimed 2–10s lag. [Indie Hackers post (2026)](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/latency-arbitrage-in-15-minute-crypto-markets-building-a-polymarket-trading-edge-2026-f77cc226c0) covers the same. Note these are VPS/marketing-adjacent sources; the true post-fee lag today is unknown, but the January 2026 dynamic fee **[V]** was introduced precisely because this worked — Finance Magnates reports one wallet turned **$313 into ~$414k in one month** on thousands of near-50/50 latency trades ([source](https://www.financemagnates.com/cryptocurrency/polymarket-introduces-dynamic-fees-to-curb-latency-arbitrage-in-short-term-crypto-markets/)); [Polybot Arena](https://polybot-arena.com/blog/how-polymarket-bots-trade/) cites the same wallet as $313→$438k **[C]**.
- **[C] Last-second dynamics** ([BenjaminCup, Medium](https://benjamincup.medium.com/unlocking-edges-in-polymarkets-5-minute-crypto-markets-last-second-dynamics-bot-strategies-and-db8efcb5c196)): claims 15–20% of 5m periods are decided by the final-10-second move; Chainlink updates every ~10–30s or on 0.5% deviation while resolution uses the exact end-timestamp snapshot; Polygon confirmation latency (2–5s) pushes practical entries to the final 15–30s. Claims 55–60% win rate backtests for last-minute momentum entry with >5–10% edge threshold.
- **[C] Bot behavior tracking**: [Polybot Arena](https://polybot-arena.com/blog/how-polymarket-bots-trade/) profiles live bots: median hold ~142 seconds, mean-reversion "buy dips sell peaks", multi-timeframe (5m/15m/hourly) participation, documented cases of ~98% win rates and $13k+/day P&L since Feb 2026.
- **[C] Passive both-sides farming**: [HTX Insights "86% return"](https://www.htx.com/news/86-return-how-to-use-a-bot-to-earn-passively-on-polymarket-1lvJrZQN/) and [dev.to arb-bot tutorial](https://dev.to/chudi_nnorukam/how-i-built-a-polymarket-trading-bot-that-actually-makes-money-1fb9) popularize sum<$1 rebalancing arb to a mass audience — a strong sign that edge is saturated.

## (a) Crowded edges — assume arbitraged away (or fee-taxed away) in our data

1. **Binance→Polymarket latency arbitrage near 50/50.** The single most publicized edge; explicitly targeted and largely killed by the Jan 2026 dynamic taker fee (~3.15% at midpoint). Expect it to be visible and profitable in pre-2026 data, gone after. **[V]**
2. **Up+Down < $1 rebalancing arbitrage.** Tutorialized on dev.to, HTX, Medium; wallet examples published; academic paper quantifies it. Whatever remains is a speed race. **[V/C]**
3. **Near-resolution sniping (buy the ~0.99 sure thing).** Named bot archetype with example wallets; also excluded from liquidity rewards (>98c trades), so the platform already prices it in. **[C]**
4. **Cross-timeframe 5m↔15m lag capture.** Public archetype; also out of our scope (needs the other market's feed). **[C]**
5. **Dump-and-hedge / short-vol DCA under 35c ("gabagool").** Multiple public writeups, claimed by many bots; 95–98% win-rate short-vol profiles get crowded fast and blow up on tails. **[C]**
6. **TA-indicator momentum off Binance spot (RSI/VWAP/MACD).** The most-starred 15m repos all do this; if it's in a 700-star README, it's in the price. **[V repos, C efficacy]**

## (b) Underexplored angles — ranked, book-stream-only compatible

Every angle below uses only the market's own recorded order-book stream (fits [`strategy-research-protocol/SCOPE.md`](../../SCOPE.md)). Rationale for "underexplored": absent from all public repos/writeups found, which are uniformly anchored on external price feeds.

1. **Aggressor-burst inference (the book as a proxy BTC feed).** The fastest players hit these books precisely when Binance moves; their taker sweeps arrive seconds before slower participants reprice. Detecting directional taker bursts / multi-level sweeps in our own stream recovers the external signal _from inside the book_ — then trade the follow-through against the slow crowd. No public bot does this; they all subscribe to Binance directly and ignore what flow itself reveals.
2. **Maker-withdrawal as a volatility/direction signal.** poly-maker-style bots pull or widen quotes when their toxicity/regime logic fires. Depth evaporation and cancel-rate spikes on one side are therefore a _leading_ indicator of an incoming move — observable purely from book deltas. Public discussion of quote-pulling exists only from the MM's perspective, never as a signal to trade against.
3. **Fee-shadow microstructure (post-Jan-2026 regime).** The dynamic taker fee reshapes behavior near 50/50: taking is uneconomic mid-range, so flow bunches at prices where fee < edge. This creates predictable dead zones, queue dynamics, and imbalance patterns that didn't exist pre-fee — and every public strategy writeup predates or ignores the fee change. Our post-Jan-2026 book data can map where the fee pushes flow and position passively on the profitable side of it.
4. **Retail-timing exploitation.** Bloomberg's finding — retail picks winners more often but loses by entering late at bad prices — implies a detectable late-window, small-size, price-insensitive flow cohort. Providing liquidity selectively when flow signatures look retail (small, sparse, marketable) vs stepping aside when they look bot (fast, repeated, sweep-like) is a book-only adverse-selection filter. Public MM bots quote symmetrically and only crudely detect toxicity.
5. **Window-lifecycle seams.** Each 15m market has a birth (thin, wide, anchor-less book) and a death (resolution scramble). Public attention is all on the final seconds (taker side); the _opening_ minutes — how the first quotes anchor, who seeds the book, systematic early mispricing vs eventual mid — appear completely unexamined publicly and are fully observable in our stream.
6. **Wash/rewards-flow filtering as alpha hygiene.** Given ~25% of platform volume may be non-informational (rewards/airdrop farming), classifying and excluding farming-signature prints from our signals (self-crossing patterns, sub-penny churn, reward-window periodicity) should sharpen every other signal — a data-quality edge no public bot writeup mentions.
