# Mission 01 — Explore

> status: DRAFT — the human is still refining this; it becomes law when he
> marks it `active`.

## Goal

Learn our system hands-on, prove it by measuring, and leave behind the
foundations the team will work on: tools, verified knowledge, and a proposal
for how the team should work.

## What to do

1. **Explore the engine hands-on** — backtesting, the fleet, the DB, the
   dashboard, the dataset. Not by reading only: run things, measure, verify.
2. **Write `ENGINE.md` from scratch** — an AI-format digest of what THIS
   protocol needs to know about the engine, with citations into `docs/` and
   source code. No inherited material; every claim verified by you.
3. **Build your first tools** — whatever you find yourself needing (running
   backtests, reading results, inspecting runs). Tools useful to others go
   in the shared space.
4. **Prove the pipeline end-to-end** — a smoke strategy (`pair-<you>-smoke`)
   through the fleet → DB → results read back, so the whole loop is verified
   before real research starts.
5. **Re-verify the speed anchor** — RULES quotes ~1.5 s/market/slot as an
   old measurement; measure it fresh and correct RULES via the human if it
   is off.
6. **Propose the team's working system** — how work is divided, how
   experiments are recorded, how knowledge is shared (see VISION "Starting
   proposal" for one option; you may design better). Written as a proposal
   document for the other model and the human to review.

## Done when

- ENGINE.md exists and every claim in it is verified with a citation or a
  run id.
- The smoke strategy has gone through the full loop with results read back.
- The working-system proposal is written and the human has reviewed it.

(The human refines these criteria before activation.)
