## What happened on 2026-10-09, in order

1. Morning: attempt 2's lead ran its first night on the wrong plan tier and
   lost most of it to rate limits; resumed on the $200 plan at 11:37 and
   produced ~150k lines across 14 parallel workstreams in about three hours.
2. Afternoon: an independent review of attempt 2's plan found it was three
   projects in one, that its speed projections disagreed with each other and
   rested on an unmeasured number, that the copy-mode profile pulled TS
   structure into the core, and that the only ground truth (the exchange) came
   last. Two amendments were committed there (D70: live and calibration to a
   later goal; D71: copy mode trimmed).
3. The owner then concluded the order of work itself was backwards and chose
   a new goal: exchange and Recorder V4 first, the TS engine as a golden
   generator only, a short spec, one defined step at a time. Attempt 3 was
   bootstrapped from main in about 75 minutes, reviewed by an independent
   agent (38 findings, the blocking ones fixed), and the milestones were cut
   to what is actually defined.
4. Evening: two visual pages mapping the TypeScript code to the plan were
   produced from seven read-only passes over the code on main.
