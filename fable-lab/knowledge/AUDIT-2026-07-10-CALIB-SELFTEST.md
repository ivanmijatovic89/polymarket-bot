The expected string is a prefix of any corrupted longer line: a hypothetical calib.ts bug that pushed the demoted cell into `candidates` in ADDITION to `negFlags` (double-listing; e.g. a missing `else`) would produce "CANDIDATE cells: UP (30s, ...)[...], demoted UP (150s, ...)" and still pass all 22 assertions, because the per-row flag regexes (:187) and negFlags string (:191) are independently satisfied. Anchoring to end-of-line (regex `$`) would close this.
