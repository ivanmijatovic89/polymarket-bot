## Findings — discrepancies

1. **MATERIAL — the "pre-results" claim on the U37 amendments is mechanically false for EXP-008.** `knowledge/AUDIT-2026-07-10-D18-UNLOCK.md` (preamble) states the fixes were applied before results were read, "mechanically checkable: the fix commit predates the probes' completion timestamps in `logs/touch-probes.log`" — and both spec amendment headers say "appended while the probe was still running; no probe statistic had been read". The log's UTC stamps show EXP-008 completed at 05:55:40Z (start 05:44:07Z + 11m32s wall clock; EXP-009 header starts 05:55:41Z), while the amendment commit 1aec35a is 2026-07-10T07:57:36+02:00 = **05:57:36Z — 1m56s AFTER run 357's completion and persist** (EXP-009 was still running, so the claim holds for it). The stated mechanical check therefore fails for EXP-008 as written. Mitigations, all verified: the decision BARS were frozen at registration (30dc724, 05:42:37Z, before probe start) and the amendments changed no bar; every amendment moves against the researcher (weakened kill wording, added disclosure duties, added a park-not-kill contamination escape that did not fire); reading run 357's results requires an explicit `results.ts` invocation for which there is no evidence; and authoring the 226-line commit in under 2 minutes is implausible, so the content almost certainly predates completion. The kill verdicts are unaffected — they fire on the pre-launch bars alone — but the audit-trail sentence needs an erratum: for EXP-008 the no-peek property is honor-system over a ~2-minute window, not mechanically proven.
2. **MINOR — E19's "touch mode roughly doubled fill density" understates the fill-count side.** Played markets doubled (348 vs 177, 1.97×), but raw maker fills went 1482 vs 342 = 4.3×. Both raw numbers are printed in the same sentence, so nothing is hidden; the summary adjective fits only the played-market metric.
3. **NOTE — `tools/runs.ts` prints DB `createdAt` with a `Z` suffix that is actually local (+02:00) wall-clock.** Proven by cross-referencing the log's true UTC stamps (run 357 done 05:55:40Z; runs.ts shows "07:55:40.000Z"). This display quirk is exactly what makes discrepancy 1 easy to miss and could corrupt future timing audits; the log, not runs.ts, is the reliable clock.
4. **NOTE — the D18 hook forced 484/485 of ~500 BacktestExecution instances, not all.** Instances whose `onMarketTick` never fired (markets with no in-window events) stayed at `worst_queue`. Harmless by the audited hook semantics (the only maker-fill evaluation site is inside `onMarketTick`, and a strategy that never ticks places no orders — those markets show zero trades), but "N instances forced" should not be read as "all N markets".

## Unverifiable claims (by construction, with reason)

- **"Latency pinned 0/0 (D8)"** for runs 357/358: env vars are not persisted in `cmd`, the log prints no latency line, and D8's pinning runs through `submit.ts` while these probes were launched via `detach.mjs` + wrapper. Only the launch record (STATE U36) asserts it. No contrary evidence; unverifiable from durable artifacts.
- **"No probe statistic had been read" before the amendments (EXP-008 window)** — see discrepancy 1: honor-system for the final ~2 minutes; a negative (non-reading) is unprovable.
- **"No fill explainable only by a crossed book state"** (both tripwires): I verified prices, sidedness, trade counts, and pattern-consistency for all 10 inspected markets, and the EXP-009 sign argument is mechanical — but positively ruling out same-tick crossed-state fills would require replaying book states (the diag fixture), i.e., launching a backtest, which this audit is forbidden to do. The pre-registered criterion (sign of pnlTotal) is satisfied regardless, and by construction phantom fills outside the top-5 |PnL| set are out of the tripwire's pre-registered scope.
- **Run 353's per-market PnL non-inspection at registration time** (D18-audit finding 4.4): disclosed honor-system peek-abstention; permanently unverifiable.

## Verdict on the chain (sound / sound-with-errata / unsound)

**Sound-with-errata.** Every number in both verdicts, in E19, and in the EDGE-SPACE/IDEAS updates reproduces exactly from the DB and independent recomputation; both kills satisfy their own pre-registered bars twice over with the escalate and ambiguous branches correctly not taken; the samples are clean 500-draw exploration-only randoms with the boundary market provably absent (DB-level, all four runs); the frozen-cell inheritance, hook activation, tripwire inspections, parent comparisons, and charter/D18 compliance all check out; and the knowledge chain nowhere overclaims beyond audit amendment 4.1. The one required erratum: the "mechanically checkable pre-results" statement on the U37 amendments is false for EXP-008 (amendment commit 05:57:36Z vs run 357 completion 05:55:40Z) — the amendments' pre-results status for EXP-008 rests on plausibility and the conservative direction of the changes, not on commit timing, and the lab should record that correction (plus, ideally, fix runs.ts's misleading timestamp suffix). The kill conclusions themselves do not depend on the amendments and stand on the pre-launch registration.
